I'm Liwen (Robin) Zhang, a science content director and video producer based in Shanghai, China, with a research background in genomics and bioinformatics.

I received my M.S. in Biology from <a href="https://en.westlake.edu.cn/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Westlake University</a> (2025), where I worked in the <a href="https://zhangyxlab.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Computational and Functional Genomics Lab</a> advised by <a href="https://en.westlake.edu.cn/faculty/yanxiao-zhang.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Yanxiao ZHANG</a>, integrating transcriptomic, DNA methylation and single-cell multi-omics data to study cancer. I am first author of a paper in <i>Scientific Reports</i> on the subclonal heterogeneity of liver cancer. Before that, I earned my B.S. in Biology from the University of Science and Technology of China (2020), where I studied tumor regulation and epigenetics under Prof. Wen Pan and competed in iGEM with the USTC team.

Today I work as a science content director and executive producer at Aoying (Shanghai) Culture Media, directing science documentaries, brand films and live productions — including the Bilibili series《魔都科学目录》, the Midea × Xuelong brand film, and Shanghai's "Director Talks Policy" series. I previously helped build the science IP 《科技袁人》(Yuan's Multiverse), which surpassed 50 million views and 5 million followers across platforms. As the founder of the WarudoKaosu studio, I also organize live events (WRDK / IdolKingdom), serving over 10,000 attendees.

Accompanied by a cat named "Todo" (土豆). In my spare time, I love photography, rock music and football.
