"Starry Stone Story" (《星石故事》) — a meteorite-themed science video series.

- Deeply involved in early-stage planning and mid-stage filming.
- Two episodes featured in-depth interviews with meteorite researchers, filmed at the Shanghai Astronomy Museum; one episode was a documentary with a meteorite hunter filmed in the Taklamakan Desert, Xinjiang.
- 1st Prize & Most Popular Award, 2025 Shanghai Science Popularization Competition.
